2. Smartwatch vibration

When the alarm goes off your smartwatch can vibrate on your wrist. This works with almost any watch or fitness band that shows your phone's notifications (Wear OS, Galaxy Watch, Apple Watch, Mi Band, Amazfit, Noise, boAt…).

  1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone (Play Store / App Store).
  2. In the app tap + and subscribe to this topic: –
  3. In your watch's companion app (Galaxy Wearable, Zepp, Mi Fitness, …) allow notifications from ntfy.
  4. Click Send test vibration. Your phone and watch should buzz within a few seconds.

Keep the topic name private — anyone who knows it can see these alerts. Bands that support the Bluetooth "Immediate Alert" service can also be connected directly from the Monitor page.
